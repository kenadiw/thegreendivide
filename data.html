The Data Behind the Divide.

Understanding the impact of marijuana criminalization requires looking beyond legislation. Research and statistics help reveal how enforcement has affected different communities throughout the United States.

UNDERSTANDING THE NUMBERS

The Research

What Should We Be Measuring?

Looking at marijuana enforcement involves more than counting arrests. Different measurements help explain how criminalization affects individuals and communities.

Arrest Rates

Examining the number of marijuana-related arrests across different racial and demographic groups.

Enforcement Patterns

Exploring differences in how marijuana laws are enforced across locations and communities.

Long-Term Effects

Understanding how arrests and criminal records can affect employment, education, and future opportunities.

Examining Enforcement

Why Arrest Disparities Matter

Marijuana laws have affected communities differently. Comparing arrest data can help identify differences in enforcement and raise questions about fairness within the criminal justice system.

Racial Differences

Research comparing arrest rates can help identify racial disparities in marijuana enforcement, even when marijuana use rates are similar across groups.

Community Impact

Arrests can create lasting consequences for individuals, families, and communities, extending beyond the initial interaction with law enforcement.
