History of Marijuana Law

A Timeline of Marijuana Criminalization.

Follow major developments in U.S. marijuana policy and see how laws have changed over time.

Key periods and turning points

[1937]

The Marihuana Tax Act

The federal government passed legislation that placed restrictions and taxes on marijuana.

[1970]

Controlled Substances Act

Marijuana was classified as a controlled substance under federal law.

[1996]

California Legalizes Medical Marijuana

California became the first state to legalize medical marijuana through a voter initiative.

[2012]

Colorado & Washington Legalize Adult Use

Colorado and Washington became the first states to legalize recreational marijuana.
